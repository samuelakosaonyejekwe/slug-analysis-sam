--------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THIS IS, AND WHAT IT IS NOT

IS:  the first quantitative support for the SHAPE of this project's central
     argument, scored on measured outcomes from a real flowloop. An inventory
     threshold does not merely fail to separate these 12 outcomes -- at
     AUC 0.148 it separates them BACKWARDS, so a model treating plugging as
     "enough hydrate has formed" gets them wrong more often than chance.

NOT: a validation of Phi_SH, Phi_crit, C_phi or k_ero. Phi_SH needs a wall
     subcooling and a slug frequency, and Pham tabulates neither. Their
     mechanism is agglomeration in a water-continuous suspension; Phi_SH is
     wall deposition against shear removal. Two different mechanisms that
     share a structural conclusion. Kv is not Phi_SH and is not a proxy for it.

     n = 12 with 3 positives. The AUC standard error at that size is large
     (roughly +/-0.15), so 0.778 vs 0.148 is a clear ordering but neither
     number is precise. Do not quote them as accuracies.

     Scale: 1 cm bore against this case's 273 mm. Most tests carry
     anti-agglomerant and/or NaCl, which this case does not.
